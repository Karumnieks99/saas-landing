03 Method

Four weeks. Four gates. No exceptions.

01

Consultation

Week 0 · Remote or at the bench

It starts with how you work — the renders you wait on, the sims you queue, the games you actually play, the room the machine will live in. No order form. A conversation, then a written specification at a fixed price.

You receive Spec dossier · fixed quote

02

Engineering

Weeks 1–2 · Thermals, acoustics, power

Every platform is modelled before a single part is unboxed: thermal headroom at your real workloads, noise at your actual desk distance, power delivery with margin you will never touch. The loop is routed on paper before it is bent in metal.

You receive Build sheet · loop schematic · render

03

Assembly

Week 3 · One builder, one bench

Your system is assembled start to finish by a single builder. Fasteners torqued to specification, cables cut and pinned to length, every loom trained before the panel goes on. Nothing zip-tied that should be sleeved.

You receive Timestamped photo log of every stage

04

Validation

Week 4 · 72 hours under load

Seventy-two hours of soak: full-load thermals, memory stability, storage and transient-response sweeps — logged, graphed, and signed by the builder who torqued the last fastener. If a number is off, it does not ship.

You receive Signed validation sheet · lifetime tuning support

Twelve a month. The bench is open.
