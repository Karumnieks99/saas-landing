Small pill badge with four ScopeBolt roles: orange section eyebrows, emerald money-recovered proof, pulsing live status, and neutral tags.

```jsx
<Badge variant="eyebrow">How it works</Badge>
<Badge variant="money">$11,400 recovered</Badge>
<Badge variant="live">Used by electrical, mechanical & concrete subs</Badge>
<Badge variant="neutral">Procore</Badge>
```

`money` auto-prepends a check; `live` auto-prepends a pulsing emerald dot.
